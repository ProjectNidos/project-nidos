GDPR Compliance

Last updated: June 2026

1. Our commitment

The Nidos Platform is built and maintained in full compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR 2016/679). Data protection is part of our architecture, starting from the database schema.

2. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data on the following GDPR lawful bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)): account data for providing the Platform service
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): anonymised usage data for improving the platform
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): demo requests and contact forms

3. Data Processor role

When you use our services, SIA Project Nidos acts as the Data Processor. Your organisation is the Data Controller. We process your data only on your documented instructions. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is included in every service agreement.

4. Technical and organisational measures

  • All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • EU-hosted infrastructure — data does not leave the EEA
  • Role management with audit logs
  • Daily encrypted backups with 30-day retention
  • Multi-factor authentication available for all accounts
  • Regular vulnerability scanning

5. Sub-processors

We use the following sub-processors, all based in the EU:

  • Hetzner (Germany) — cloud infrastructure
  • Server-side aggregated analytics (EU) — anonymised, no personal data

6. Breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected controllers without undue delay and within 72 hours, in accordance with Article 33 of the GDPR.
