Cookie Policy

This website sets no cookies for visitors. It keeps a handful of small values in your browser so the site works as you would expect, and every one of them is listed here.

01

Cookies and browser storage

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to keep. Browsers also offer local storage and session storage, which do a similar job but are never sent to the server. The law treats all three alike (Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive), so this policy covers all three.

02

What the website stores

The public website sets no cookies. It keeps these values in your browser:

NameTypeWhat it is forKept for
cookie_consentLocal storageRemembers that you answered the cookie notice, so it does not ask againUntil you clear your browser data
pn_intro_seenSession storageSkips the opening animation once you have seen it in this tabUntil you close the tab
pn_gate_unlockedSession storageOnly while the site is in password-protected preview: remembers that you entered the passwordUntil you close the tab
pn_arcade_hsLocal storageOnly if you play the game hidden in the footer: your high scoreUntil you clear your browser data

All four are first-party, stay in your browser, and hold nothing that identifies you — our server never reads them. Each one serves something you asked for, so none needs your consent.

03

The staff sign-in area

Our CRM and admin panel are for Project Nidos staff only. Signing in to them stores:

NameTypeWhat it is forKept for
tokenCookie — HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=StrictKeeps a staff member signed in. Scripts cannot read it, and no other site's request carries it8 hours, or until sign-out
crm_userLocal storageThe signed-in staff member's name, email and role, shown in the interfaceUntil sign-out

Both are strictly necessary for signing in, and neither is used for anything else.

04

What the cookie notice does

Because everything above is strictly necessary, choosing Accept or Decline does not change what the site stores. Either way, it records your answer in cookie_consent so the notice does not come back.

If we ever add something that needs your consent, we will ask before it runs, and the site will work in full if you say no.

05

What we do not use

  • Analytics of any kind, our own or anyone else's
  • Advertising cookies or networks
  • Social media pixels, plugins or embeds
  • Fingerprinting
  • Third-party fonts or scripts — our fonts are served from our own server
06

Clearing and blocking

You can clear or block cookies and site data in your browser's settings at any time. Clearing the site data for projectnidos.eu removes everything listed on this page.

The public pages work with storage blocked; you may see the notice and the opening animation again on each visit. Signing in to the staff area needs the token cookie.
