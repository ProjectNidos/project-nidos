Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences, authenticate your session and provide basic functionality.

2. Which cookies we use

The Project Nidos public marketing website (projectnidos.eu) does not set cookies. Cookies are set only in the authenticated platform environment (for users logged in with an account):

CookiePurposeDuration
session_idAuthentication session — maintains your login stateSession (deleted when you close the browser)
csrf_tokenSecurity — protection against CSRF attacksSession
cookie_consentRemembers your cookie choice12 months

All our cookies are first-party (set only from our domain) and strictly necessary for the platform's functionality and security. They are not used for tracking, profiling or advertising.

3. What we do NOT use

We deliberately do not use:

  • Third-party tracking cookies
  • Advertising cookies or networks
  • Social media pixels or plugins
  • Fingerprinting libraries
  • Third-party analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.)

4. How we do analytics

We collect anonymised usage statistics using server-side aggregation of server access logs (request counts, breakdown by country, median time on page). This processing takes place only on our servers, sets no cookies, collects no personal data and stores only daily summaries — never per individual visitor.

5. Google Fonts

We load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) to render typography. Google Fonts requests transmit your IP address and user agent to Google LLC (USA), but do not set cookies on your device. More on how Google processes data: Google Privacy Policy.

6. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. The necessary cookies are required for the platform to work — disabling them will prevent you from logging in to the platform. The public website (projectnidos.eu) works without cookies — you can also browse it with cookies fully disabled.
