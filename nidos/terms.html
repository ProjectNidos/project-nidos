2. Services

Project Nidos provides six implementation practices:

  • CRM implementation: platform selection, sales pipeline configuration, data import, integrations and training.
  • Sales process automation: lead channels, automatic assignment, follow-up scenarios and sales KPIs.
  • Customer service automation: helpdesk, knowledge base, AI assistant, escalation and SLA monitoring.
  • Warehouse or accounting system integration: data flow audit, API and webhook connections, field mapping and an error log.
  • AI solutions for specific processes: process assessment, prototype, integration with one system and measurement of results.
  • E-commerce and payment integration: shop configuration, payment service providers, delivery and accounting flow.

Each implementation project is carried out with a fixed scope: the contract defines the features, integrations, number of users, deadlines and acceptance criteria.
