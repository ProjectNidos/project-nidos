2. What we collect

We collect only the personal data necessary to operate the website, provide the platform and respond to your requests.

Server access logs. Our web server records every request: your IP address, user agent string, the page requested, the response status and a timestamp. These logs are used to operate the website, prevent abuse and fulfil our security obligations.

Account data. When you register on the Nidos platform or request a demo: name, email address, company name, job title — only what you submit yourself.

Platform data. Compliance data that you upload to the Nidos platform: biodiversity indicators, land use records, supply chain data, monitoring results. This data is your property — we process it solely to provide the service.

Email correspondence. When you write to us at support@projectnidos.eu, we receive your email address and the content of your message. Email is delivered via Google Workspace (described in Section 4).

Contact form data. When you fill in the contact form: name, email, company and the content of your message.

First-party cookies. We use only the cookies required for functional purposes — session authentication, CSRF protection and remembering your cookie preferences. For a detailed list, see our Cookie Policy. The public marketing pages (this website) do not set cookies — they are set only in the authenticated platform area.

Server-side aggregated analytics. We process server access logs in aggregated form by day (number of requests, breakdown by country from IP geolocation, median time on page). Processing takes place server-side and only daily summaries are stored — never data on individual visitors. The aggregate is available only to the Project Nidos operations team.

We do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, advertising networks, fingerprinting libraries or any third-party scripts, except as described in Section 4.

3. Legal basis for processing (Article 6 GDPR)

We rely on the following grounds under Article 6(1) GDPR:

  • (b) Steps prior to entering into a contract — when you request a demo or contact us about a collaboration.
  • (b) Performance of a contract — when you are a platform user and we process your data to provide the service.
  • (f) Legitimate interests — for server logs (security, abuse prevention, operational integrity), aggregated analytics (platform improvement), and responding to general enquiries.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your platform data for anything other than providing the service.

4. Who we transfer data to (third parties)

Hetzner Cloud GmbH (Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany) — our hosting provider. Acts as our data processor within the meaning of Article 28 GDPR. Hetzner stores the website files, runs the server and maintains the access logs within the EU.

Google Fonts — we load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com (the service is provided by Google LLC, USA). Every page load transfers your IP address and user agent to Google. This is necessary to render the website's typography. Google's processing is governed by its privacy policy.

Google LLC (Google Workspace) (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) — our business email provider. Acts as our data processor within the meaning of Article 28 GDPR. Google receives, stores and processes email content, including the sender's address, headers, attachments and message body. EU-region data residency is configured to the extent permitted by the Workspace subscription plan.

No other third parties receive your data from us.

5. International transfers

The Google Fonts requests mentioned in Section 4 transfer your IP address and user agent to the United States of America. Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework as the legal basis for these transfers.

Email content processed via Google Workspace (Section 4) may be transferred to and stored on Google infrastructure in the USA and in other jurisdictions where Google operates. The legal basis is the EU-US Data Privacy Framework together with the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) incorporated in the Workspace Data Processing Addendum.

All other data remains within the EU.

6. Retention periods

  • Server access logs: up to 30 days, after which we delete them.
  • Platform data: kept for as long as your contract is active. After the contract ends, you have 30 days to export your data, after which it is permanently deleted.
  • Email correspondence: kept for as long as a collaboration discussion is active, plus up to 5 years after the last contact for collaboration-related records (legitimate interest in defending against claims and complying with Latvian requirements for retaining commercial documents). General enquiries that do not lead to a collaboration are deleted within 12 months.
  • Contact form data: 12 months after the last contact, unless you become a client.

7. Your rights (Articles 15–22 GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("the right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing in certain cases
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, write to support@projectnidos.eu. We will respond within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests in accordance with Article 12(3) GDPR).
