01

CRM implementation

One CRM system and a transparent sales pipeline.

Project Nidos helps companies select, configure and implement a CRM system that brings together customer data, sales processes, tasks, communication and sales analytics.

Client problemCustomer data and sales work are spread across different locations and systems.

Scope of service

  • current sales process audit
  • CRM platform selection
  • customer and company data structure
  • sales pipeline configuration
  • user permissions and roles
  • email, calendar and form integrations
  • existing data import
  • automated tasks and notifications
  • sales reports and KPI dashboards
  • employee training
  • documentation and post-implementation support

Recommended entry package

CRM Start

Up to five users, one sales pipeline, up to ten custom fields, email integration, up to 500 importable records, two training sessions and 14 days of post-implementation support.

Licensing is not included.

  • follow-up reminders

  • calendar booking
  • sales forecasting
  • lost-deal analysis
  • CRM, email, Forms, WhatsApp and other tool integrations

Warehouse or accounting system integration

Synchronised orders, inventory, invoices and customer data.

Project Nidos connects warehouse, accounting, e-commerce, CRM and payment systems so that data does not have to be entered multiple times.

Client problemThe same data has to be entered into multiple systems.

Typical integration scenarios

  • online store with warehouse system
  • online store with accounting software
  • CRM with invoicing system
  • ERP with payment service provider
  • warehouse with delivery provider
  • orders with automatic invoice generation
  • inventory levels with online store stock updates
  • returns with accounting records
  • data synchronisation through APIs, webhooks or automation platforms

Recommended entry package

Business Systems Integration

An audit of existing systems and data flows, design of one primary integration scenario, API or webhook connection, data-field mapping, error log, testing, documentation and 30 days of warranty support.

  • inventory and stock synchronisation

  • returns and refunds process
  • multilingual and multi-currency support
  • analytics, conversion tracking and GDPR requirements

Commerce & Payments Launch
